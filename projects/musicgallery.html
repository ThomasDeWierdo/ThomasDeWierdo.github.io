Music

 Another thing that I've been enjoying when it comes to game development is music composition. I have been making video game music for around 3 years now, and I think I've improved significantly since when I first started, and am still improving with every song. I started by using a free website called soundtrap, then a little under a year ago, I bought a professional program called FL Studio and have made around 20 songs since then. I've been making these songs mainly just to get better at making music, learning what makes a song sound good, what makes one bad, so most of them aren't targeted toward a specific part of gameplay for my game. I've selected a variety of my best songs to show here.
